## Overview

Email Connector is designed not just for developers, but for store owners, small and medium businesses (SMBs), and non-technical founders. Our ready-to-use apps and plugins bring enterprise-grade email failover and cost optimization to standard business software with zero code required.

---

## 1. Shopify Store Connector

Never lose revenue to dropped order receipts or abandoned cart recovery emails:
- **Automatic Fallback:** If SendGrid or your primary Shopify email app experiences an outage, transactional emails automatically send via your backup provider.
- **Order Confirmations:** 100% deliverability for receipts, shipping updates, and refund notifications.
- **Setup in 2 Minutes:** Install via the Shopify App Store, enter your provider keys, and let Email Connector manage delivery.

---

## 2. WooCommerce & WordPress Plugin

WordPress `wp_mail()` is notorious for delivery failures and silent server drops:
- **Replaces `wp_mail()`:** Intercepts all WordPress emails and routes them through the Email Connector zero-trust engine.
- **Combined Free Tiers:** Perfect for small stores sending up to 800 emails/day completely free.
- **Installation:**
  1. Download `email-connector-wp.zip` from your dashboard.
  2. Upload to **Plugins > Add New** in WordPress admin.
  3. Enter your email provider credentials and click **Test Zero-Downtime Delivery**.

---

## 3. Webflow & Framer Form Bridge

Route web form submissions directly into your zero-downtime email cascade:
- **Webhook Endpoint:** Point your Webflow form action to `https://gateway.email-connector.com/v1/forms/bridge`.
- **Instant Notification:** Receives form payload and dispatches notifications across your backup servers.
