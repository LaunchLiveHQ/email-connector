# Email Connector

## Why Email Connector?

### 1. Direct Sending With No Middleman
Unlike other email platforms, Email Connector connects directly from your own application to your email provider.
- **Direct Sending:** Emails are delivered straight from your website or server to Brevo, Resend, Mailjet, MailerSend, AWS SES, or SendGrid.
- **Complete Privacy:** Customer emails, personal details, and provider API keys are never stored on, inspected by, or routed through any third-party middleman.

### 2. Built-in Priority Cascades & Quota Failover
Configure servers by priority level (1 to 5). If Priority 1 provider hits its daily free limit (e.g. Resend's 100/day limit) or encounters a rate limit (HTTP 429), the engine automatically cascades to Priority 2 (e.g. Brevo's 300/day limit) without dropping a single email.

### 3. Interactive Forms & Lead Engine
Deploy pre-built lead capture and survey forms with step-by-step conversational runners, lead ledger inspections, CSV exports, and automatic email delivery backup.

### 4. Ready-to-Use Plugins for Store Owners
Ready-to-use apps for Shopify, WooCommerce/WordPress, and Webflow ensure eCommerce stores never drop receipt emails or order notifications.

### 5. High-Performance Go Gateway & MCP Server (`apps/gateway-go`)
Includes a production-grade Go HTTP gateway with RFC 5322 MIME stream parsing, reply-chain stripping, automated OTP extraction, and a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server for autonomous AI workflows.
