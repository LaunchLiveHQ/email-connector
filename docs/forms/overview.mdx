## Overview

Email Connector Forms is an interactive, step-by-step conversational form and lead capture service. It allows founders, marketers, and developers to deploy branded forms in seconds, capture contact information, and ensure every form submission triggers instant, zero-downtime email notifications.

---

## Why Use Email Connector Forms?

1. **Guaranteed Delivery:** When someone submits your form, notifications route through your configured backup email providers. If one provider fails, your emails automatically send through your backup service.
2. **Pre-Built Templates:** Choose from ready-to-use templates:
   - **Contact & Inquiries:** Collect visitor inquiries with name, email, company, and message.
   - **Customer Feedback & NPS:** Gather structured ratings and open feedback.
   - **Event & Webinar Registration:** Capture attendee information with automated confirmations.
   - **Newsletter Lead Capture:** Grow your audience with clean, mobile-first signup forms.
3. **Share or Embed Anywhere:**
   - **Standalone Links:** Share your direct form link (`https://email-connector.com/forms/[id]`).
   - **Embeddable React Component:** Embed directly in your Next.js or React application using `<FormRunner />`.
4. **Transparent Pricing & 7-Day Free Trial:**
   - Starter, Growth, and Enterprise plans with a 7-day risk-free trial.
   - Quotas, custom domains, and responses are manageable directly from the dashboard.

## Embedding in Your Application

Install the `@emailconnector/react` package:

```bash
npm install @emailconnector/react
```

Embed the form runner in your component:

```tsx
import { FormRunner } from "@emailconnector/react";

export function ContactWidget() {
  return (
    <div className="max-w-2xl mx-auto p-4">
      <FormRunner
        formId="contact_general"
        onComplete={(submission) => {
          console.log("Form submitted successfully:", submission);
        }}
      />
    </div>
  );
}
```

## Form Submission Telemetry

Every submission is recorded in the platform ledger:
- **Instant Notification:** Immediate dispatch to your team's inbox.
- **CSV & CRM Export:** Download responses anytime in `.csv` format or stream to webhooks.
- **Submission Answer Breakdown:** Review individual question responses and respondent metadata.
